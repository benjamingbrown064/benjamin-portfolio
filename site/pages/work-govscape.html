The project

Evidence, not opinion. Nothing reaches a score, a figure or a report until a person has approved it.

Scores are computed from approved evidence, accepted clause mappings and confirmed inputs. Never from anything a person has not signed off. Every engine figure starts as a draft. Approval is a hard gate.

The build runs to 79 data models, around 310 API routes, 60 screens and 395 framework clauses. It is live with a paying customer, whose governance score moved from 4 to 13 after they approved 113 evidence items themselves on 6 November.

Scheduled automation layer (12 jobs)

Cron routes handle snapshotting, score recalculation, policy extraction, identity posture, licence expiry, rescans, scan resumption, digests, quality profiling, storage reconciliation and purge of deleted organisations. The product does meaningful work with nobody signed in, which is what separates it from a form.

LLM guardrails

A guard layer sits between the application and the models with input preprocessing, output validation, budget caps and a model registry. Extraction budgets and failure handling are explicit, so a long document degrades gracefully instead of silently truncating — a real defect of this kind was found and fixed where two stacked caps were hiding 245 of 395 clauses from the extractor.

Performance work at real volume

Inventory indexes, batched review decisions and query restructuring were done against a 437,000-item live tenant. One change removed roughly 1,500 redundant whole-organisation reads from a single bulk approval.

Server-side PDF generation

A full React-PDF pipeline with charts, branding, logo fetching, figure ledgers and citation printing. Five distinct report types share one layout system.

Journal programme

01ResearchMarket and competitor research including a Vanta benchmark appraisal, sector-by-sector positioning for six verticals, and governance-risk calibration research behind the severity ratings. The calibration work is what lets the product put a defensible number on a gap rather than a colour.
02Business managementA claims register recording every factual assertion the product and site make with its source, plus a live offer definition, pricing position and roadmap. The claims register is the governance of the marketing itself — nothing goes on the site that is not traceable.
03GrowthPartner programme design with referral codes and lead attribution, sector landing-page strategy, and a journal content programme. Growth is built into the product surface, not bolted on as a channel.
04OnboardingCost-led self-serve onboarding with a live preview, a guided first-run path, activation event tracking and a first-value story designed around getting a figure on screen in minutes. The design question it answers is "what is the smallest amount of input that produces something worth paying for".
05Launch gating and acceptanceA formal launch-gate plan, acceptance evidence, a one-action acceptance test and a signed-off production re-walk. Shipping was treated as a gate with evidence, not a deploy.
06Live customer validationA real customer ran the product unprompted and approved 113 evidence items in three minutes the morning after that capability shipped, moving their score from 4 to 13.

Lead generation

A contact enquiry endpoint with notification, a referral-code system, partner-registered leads with attribution, an admin lead pipeline, and a demo path that lets a prospect see a populated product without a sales call. Attribution is recorded at first touch, so the partner channel can be measured rather than estimated.

Agentic customer support

An AI agent works the live support queue: it reads what has changed since it last looked, diagnoses the request against the real system, and writes notes, status changes, proposals and fixes — all attributed to the agent. It cannot email a customer without a human approval recorded since the last reply, and one approval sends exactly one reply, which is the entire safety design.

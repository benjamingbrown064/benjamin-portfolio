Skills

Ten questions, asked in order.

Most businesses can tell something is wrong. Fewer can say what it is, or where to fix it first. So I look at the whole business, one area at a time, in the order each part depends on the last. Seventy skills sit behind the answers.

11 / Where to start

Which answers were weakest?

Not a skill area. It is the outcome of the ten steps: the order the work should happen in. Discovery order is not delivery order, so the work starts wherever the answers were weakest.

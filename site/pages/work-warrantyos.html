WarrantyOS · Case study

Eight people. 162 dealers.

WarrantyOS lets a dealership run its own warranty programme in one app, and keep the margin an administrator would have taken. A team of eight now looks after 21,000 customers across 162 dealers.

The project

The dealer leads. We endorse. Every policy, claim and certificate in the dealer's name.

Most dealerships hand their warranty to an administrator, and the margin, the data and the customer go with it. WarrantyOS keeps all three in the showroom. The premium stays with the dealer, and WarrantyOS charges a fee per policy. Customers mostly meet it second-hand, as a certificate attached to an email from their dealer.

The handover. Where the warranty is sold.

How it works

Sold in the showroom. Kept in the showroom.

The dealer registers the policy, decides the claim and keeps the customer. WarrantyOS does the checking, the paperwork and the chasing.

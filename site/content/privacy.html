This site is run by Benjamin Brown. This page says what it collects when you use it, why, how long it is kept and how to have it removed. Questions go to hello@benjaminbrown.co.

What is collected

  • Requests you send. The contact form, the discovery questionnaire and the coffee booking collect what you type: your name, email, company, your answers and any files or links you attach.
  • Conversations with the assistant. The assistant on this site is an AI called Toto. What you write or say to it is stored so the conversation can continue and so a request can be passed to Ben.
  • Newsletter sign-ups. Your email address, and the date you confirmed it.
  • Visit statistics. Page views, the referring site, and the type of device and country, measured with Google Analytics. Unless you accept analytics cookies, this is counted without cookies and without anything that identifies you. If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies so it can tell a returning visit from a new one.

Why

To reply to you, to prepare for a conversation you asked for, to send a newsletter you signed up for, and to see which pages are useful. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

AI on this site

The assistant is software, not a person, and says so. Its answers are generated by AI models and can be wrong. It cannot agree prices, dates or contracts; those come from Ben. Ben's own assistant also helps sort incoming requests and email and drafts replies, which Ben approves before they are sent.

Who else handles it

The site relies on a small number of suppliers who process data on its behalf: Vercel (hosting), Supabase (database and file storage), Anthropic, OpenAI and ElevenLabs (the AI models behind the assistant, its voice and turning speech into text), Google (email, calendar and visit statistics), Resend (outgoing email) and Cloudflare (spam protection). Some of them process data in the United States. Text sent to the AI suppliers is used to produce the reply and is not used by this site to train models.

How long it is kept

  • Assistant conversations: 90 days, unless they became a request.
  • Requests, discovery answers and bookings: for as long as there is a working relationship, and up to two years after the last contact.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: until you unsubscribe.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising cookies. Analytics cookies from Google Analytics are set only if you accept them when asked, and you can change that choice at any time. Separately, your browser stores a few preferences on your own device: light or dark theme, pounds or dollars, a discovery questionnaire in progress, and your conversation with the assistant so it survives a page change. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to how it is used. Email hello@benjaminbrown.co and it will be dealt with within 30 days. Every newsletter has a one-click unsubscribe link. If you are in the UK or the EU you can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.

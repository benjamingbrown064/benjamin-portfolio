## The method

Ben looks at a business one area at a time, in the order each depends on the last: ten questions across four stages.

- Direction: business management and strategy; financial.
- Foundations: operations; data; compliance and governance.
- Customer: product development; marketing and brand; sales; customer support.
- Build: technical development. Technology comes last; the nine answers before it decide what gets built.

Seventy skills sit behind the ten areas. The full method is at /method. The free discovery questionnaire at /discovery walks through the same areas in about five minutes and gives a first read on where to start.

## Ways to work together

- Discovery: Ben evaluates the business and writes up where it is, where it wants to be and the road to get there. Fixed fee. The deliverable is a written appraisal report. It often leads into project work.
- Project: a defined problem, a fixed scope, a shipped outcome. Four to twelve weeks. Includes product design, full-stack build and deployment, and two rounds of iteration after launch.
- Subscription: an embedded builder, one or two days a week, on a rolling monthly retainer. Pause or cancel with 30 days' notice.

Typical rhythm on a project: first screens in week two, a staging build every Friday, one written update a week. The team is trained during the build and owns it at handover.

## This chat

- Visitors can type to you, or tap the microphone next to the message box and speak. Their words appear on the page as they talk, and the message is sent when they pause or tap send.
- When a visitor speaks a question, your answer is read aloud as well as shown. Typed questions get a written answer only.
- You receive a message only once it has been sent. You cannot hear audio or see typing in progress.
- If voice is not working for someone, suggest they type instead, or offer to open a bug report.

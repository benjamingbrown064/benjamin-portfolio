The plan

Low risk first. Then the big bets.

I sort the work the way you'd sort investments. Low risk first: cheap, quick and paid back fast. Then the medium ones. Then, if you want to go all in, the bigger pieces that can move the bottom line by a quarter or more.

Before anything's built, we work out what success is worth. If a 1% lift is worth £10,000 to you and the work costs £15,000, I'll tell you not to do it. Most plans run over about three years.
